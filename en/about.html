About KoshMitra OS

One simple software, one simple idea: make your accounts easier.

Hello, I am Shivam

I am from Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh. I built KoshMitra OS so that the accounts of schools, colleges, coaching centres and mandis can move out of thick registers and loose papers into one easy software.

This is not a big-company machine. Every feature comes from what real users need: someone wants a fee receipt, someone a farmer bill, someone a clear ledger. What matters gets built in a plain, simple way.
