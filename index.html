About us

Software made for how Indian institutes and mandis really work

KoshMitra OS is a cloud operating system for institutions and market businesses. It started with one aim: a fee or a bill should be entered once and then be available everywhere it is needed, in the right language, on any phone.

We build it in Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh, and add features based on what schools, colleges and commission agents ask for. We keep the screens simple, the reports clear, and help one WhatsApp message away.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is KoshMitra OS?

KoshMitra OS is a cloud ERP. One software for schools, colleges, universities, coaching institutes and mandi (commission agent) business: fees, bills, admission, attendance, reports and more.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. Ask for a free demo on WhatsApp and we will show you the software on your own phone.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser. On a phone you can add it to the home screen and it opens like an app.

Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Odia, Nepali, Assamese, Maithili, Manipuri, Kashmiri and Sanskrit.

Is my data kept separate from other institutes?

Yes. Each institute works inside its own account, and each person can open only the pages you allow.

Can I choose only the modules I need?

Yes. Modules can be switched on or off for each institute, so you use only what you need.

Can I bring my old student list?

Student lists can be imported from an Excel file, with photos. Tell us about your case and we will guide you.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The pages are made for phones as well as computers, and parents and students can use their own portals from a phone.

Can parents and students log in?

Yes. There is a parent portal and a student portal for fees, notices and homework.

What does the Mandi ERP cover?

Farmer and buyer records, Kisan parchi, farmer and buyer bills, packing, ledgers, stock, statements and reports for a commission agent.

How is the price decided?

It is a yearly plan that depends on the type of institute and the modules you choose. Message us for the price.

How do I get help?

Message us on WhatsApp or write an email. We reply and help you set things up.
